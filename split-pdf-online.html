Split a PDF now

Splitting a long PDF into sections — chapters, invoices, individual forms bundled into one scan — is a one-step operation in Proofmark's Page Manager. Upload the file, tell it where to split (e.g. "after page 3, after page 7"), and download a ZIP with each section as its own PDF.

Split, or extract just a few pages

If you only need specific pages rather than clean sections, the same workspace supports checking individual pages and downloading just those — see page extraction below.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose where to split?

Enter the page numbers after which you want a break, separated by commas — e.g. "3,7" splits after page 3 and after page 7, giving you three files.

What if I don't enter any split points?

The tool treats the whole document as one group, so specifying at least one split point is what actually divides the file.

Do I get one file or several?

Several — each section downloads as part of one ZIP file.
