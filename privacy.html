Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Your files never leave your device

Proofmark's watermarking, cropping, compression, conversion, and PDF tools run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. When you upload a photo, PDF, or video, that file is processed locally on your device — it is never sent to, stored on, or seen by any Proofmark server, because Proofmark does not have a server that handles files at all. If you close the tab, everything you uploaded is gone.

This is an architectural fact, not a policy promise we're asking you to trust — you can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and every tool will keep working.

What we do collect

Separately from file processing, this site uses two optional third-party services if enabled:

  • Google Analytics — collects anonymized usage data (pages visited, general location from IP, device/browser type) to help us understand how the site is used. Google Analytics does not receive your uploaded files or any watermark text/content you enter.
  • Google AdSense — may display ads and use cookies to personalize them based on your browsing activity across sites. AdSense operates independently of Proofmark's file-processing tools.

Both of these are controlled by the cookie consent banner shown on your first visit. If you decline, neither service loads.

Local storage

If you save a watermark "preset" in the tool, it's stored in your browser's local storage on your own device — not on a server. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

Third-party libraries

The tool uses a small number of open-source JavaScript libraries (for ZIP creation, PDF editing, PDF rendering, and video processing). These are self-hosted as part of this site's own files rather than loaded from a third-party CDN at request time — so no external server is even contacted to load the tool itself, beyond the optional analytics/ads described above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact details listed on our homepage.
