Sign a PDF now

For a quick document sign-off — not a legally certified digital signature, but a genuine handwritten mark on the page — Proofmark lets you draw your signature directly on a canvas, pick which page it goes on, drag it into position, and bake it permanently into the PDF.

This isn't certificate-based e-signing with an audit trail; if you need that level of legal verification, a dedicated e-signature service is the right tool. This is for the common case of "sign this and send it back."

Frequently asked questions

Is this a legally binding digital signature?

No — it places a drawn image of your signature onto the page, similar to signing a printed page and scanning it. It does not include certificate-based verification or an audit trail.

Can I choose which page to sign?

Yes — a page thumbnail strip lets you pick any page in the document before placing your signature.

Can I resize or reposition the signature?

Yes — drag it anywhere on the page and adjust its size with a slider before exporting.
