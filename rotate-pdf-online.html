Rotate a PDF now

Scanned documents often come in with a page or two rotated the wrong way. Proofmark's Page Manager shows every page as a thumbnail with its own rotate button, so you can fix just the pages that need it — no need to rotate the entire file if only one page is wrong.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate just one page instead of the whole document?

Yes — rotation is per-page. Click a page's rotate button to cycle it through 90-degree increments independently of the rest of the document.

Does rotating affect the PDF's text or quality?

No — rotation is a page-level property in the PDF format itself, so content stays exactly as it was, just displayed at a different orientation.
