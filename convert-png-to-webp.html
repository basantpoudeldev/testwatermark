Convert PNG to WebP

WebP files are typically 25–35% smaller than an equivalent PNG at similar visual quality — useful for faster-loading websites and smaller uploads.

Convert PNG to WebP now

PNG is a lossless format, which makes it larger than it needs to be for photos and complex images. WebP supports both lossless and lossy compression with generally better efficiency than PNG or JPEG at the same quality level, which is why most modern websites prefer it for images that don't need PNG's transparency-heavy, flat-graphic use case.

Other formats supported

The same Convert tool also handles PNG↔JPEG, JPEG↔WebP, and any other combination between JPEG, PNG, and WebP — with optional compression by quality percentage or an exact target file size, independent of the format conversion itself.

Frequently asked questions

Does converting to WebP lose transparency?

No — WebP supports alpha transparency the same way PNG does, so transparent areas in your PNG are preserved in the WebP output.

Can I convert many images at once?

Yes, batch conversion is supported and results are bundled into a ZIP file.
