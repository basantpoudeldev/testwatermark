Compare two PDFs now

Spotting what changed between two versions of a contract, report, or form is slow to do by eye. Proofmark renders both PDFs page by page side by side, and runs a text-level comparison on each pair of pages, highlighting additions in green and removals in red-strikethrough beneath the visual preview.

Frequently asked questions

How are pages matched between the two files?

By position — page 1 of file A is compared against page 1 of file B, and so on. If a page is inserted or removed partway through one file, later comparisons will shift out of alignment relative to the other file.

Does this detect visual-only changes, like formatting?

The side-by-side thumbnails let you visually compare layout, but the automated highlighting is text-based — purely visual changes with identical text won't be flagged by the diff.
