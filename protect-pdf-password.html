Protect a PDF now

Sharing something sensitive over email often calls for a password-protected PDF. Proofmark can add an open-password to any PDF, or remove one from a file where you already know the password — all without the file ever leaving your device.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of protection does this add?

An open password, meaning the PDF can't be viewed without entering it — the standard PDF encryption most readers (Adobe Acrobat, browser PDF viewers, etc.) recognize.

Can I restrict printing or copying without a password to open the file?

That level of granular permission control isn't included in this tool — it focuses on the open-password case, which covers the most common need.
