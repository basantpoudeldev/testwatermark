Edit a PDF now

Need to annotate a PDF, add a note, sketch a shape, or drop a logo onto a page? Proofmark's PDF editor lets you add text boxes (with your choice of font size and color), draw rectangles, circles, and lines, sketch freehand, or place an image — all directly on top of the original page, then permanently baked into the PDF on export.

What this tool is (and isn't)

This adds new content on top of a PDF page — it does not let you click into and rewrite text that's already part of the original document. Editing existing PDF text in place is a much deeper technical problem; this tool covers the common case of annotating, marking up, or adding new content to a document.

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit text that's already in the PDF?

No — this tool adds new text, images, and shapes on top of the page. It doesn't modify text that already exists in the original document.

Can I change the color and size of what I add?

Yes — text has adjustable font size and color, and shapes have adjustable stroke color and thickness.

Can I add a freehand signature or sketch?

Yes — freehand drawing is supported directly on the page in addition to typed text and shapes.
