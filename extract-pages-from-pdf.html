Frequently asked questions

Can I extract pages from multiple PDFs into one file?

Yes — upload multiple PDFs, and pages from all of them appear in the same grid, so you can select pages across files and combine them into one output.

Do extracted pages keep their original order?

They export in the order they appear in the grid, which you can also drag to rearrange before exporting.
